Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Austin Trigueiro collects, uses, and protects personal information in connection with SMS lead alert notifications.

What data is collected

When a business owner requests lead alerts, we collect:

  • The business owner's name, phone number, and email address, provided when they request the alerts.
  • Lead information submitted through a Facebook lead form for that business, including the lead's name, phone number, street address, and house size / price-check answer, which is used only to compose the alert message.

We do not collect any other personal information through the SMS alert service.

How data is used

Collected data is used only to:

  • Send SMS lead alerts to the business owner who requested them, so they can promptly contact a new lead;
  • Operate, maintain, and troubleshoot the alert service;
  • Comply with applicable law.

SMS opt-in data

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

SMS opt-in data is shared only with the messaging provider needed to deliver the alerts (Twilio) and with the business owner receiving the alert, and only for the purpose of delivering that alert. It is not shared with any other third party for marketing.

Data retention

Lead alert data is retained only as long as needed to deliver the alert and to troubleshoot delivery, and is then deleted or anonymized.

Your choices

A business owner receiving alerts may opt out at any time by replying STOP to an alert message. Reply HELP for help. Message frequency varies based on the number of leads received. Message and data rates may apply.
